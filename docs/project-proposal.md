# Project Proposal

## Target

I will design and test a high-performance, versatile mesh radio device. It will improve on two engineering fields compared to existing devices:

1. Radio-frequency (RF) and antenna design.
2. Low-power design.

These improvements will be field tested by community members to evaluate their real-world impact.

## Rationale

Everyday wireless devices like phones rely on base stations to communicate. These are single points of failure that can fail due to man-made or natural incidents (for example, a power cut), or be completely absent in remote locations.

In contrast, mesh networks such as Meshtastic are not reliant on any single device, because the mesh radio devices ("nodes") can communicate directly with each other. Distributing these devices to a community makes it more resilient, as it ensures coordination and communication can be maintained independently of major networks.

I already own many Meshtastic radio devices. They are inexpensive and functional, but there are two key problems:

1. The actual RF performance is often lacking, with poorly tuned antennas and suboptimal circuit design. This diminishes the range, efficiency, and therefore usefulness of the device.
2. Most nodes are power-hungry, with processors and high-power consumers such as GPS limiting battery life. Additionally, support for sustainable power sources (for example, solar and wind) is limited, hindering their off-grid potential.

I aim to improve on these two areas while maintaining an affordable price point compared with expensive professional nodes. The finished nodes will be sent to selected Meshtastic community members for testing.

## Timescale

1. **Design and ordering of first revision (electronics and casing):** 2 weeks.
2. **Standalone antenna and casing development:** 2 weeks, while waiting for designed parts to arrive.
3. **Testing, characterisation, and tuning of first revision:** 2 weeks.
4. **Improve design and order second revision:** 1 week.
5. **Repeat steps 2–3, distribute to the community, gather feedback, and write up:** 4 more weeks.
